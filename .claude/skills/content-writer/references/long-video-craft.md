# Long-video craft: holding viewers for 6–15 minutes (researched 2026-11-19)

Why this exists: viewers said our first long video (Dyatlov Pass, 6:59) "doesn't hold them".
Sources are mostly creator-marketing guides (numbers are rules of thumb, not measured facts),
plus how mystery documentary channels like LEMMiNO and MrBallen build their videos. Use with
`narration-craft.md` (hook, open loop, but/therefore) — this file adds what long videos need on top.

## What YouTube rewards on long videos

- Long videos and Shorts are ranked by separate models. Long-form leans on click-through rate,
  **average view duration / % watched**, whether the video extends the viewer's session, returning
  viewers, and satisfaction (surveys, "not interested").
- **% watched matters more than length.** Padding hurts. Make it as long as the story needs.
  Faceless explainer guides suggest 10–15 min; 8+ min allows mid-roll ads. Hindi mystery/true-crime
  channels that do well run 20–30 min weekly, so length is not the problem; pace is.
- Benchmarks (rough): 40–60% average view duration is excellent; under 50% of viewers still
  watching at the midpoint means the second half needs stronger material.

## Structure (beat sheet)

| Beat | Time | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0:00–0:30 | The strangest fact or scariest moment, shown, not explained. Must match title + thumbnail in the first 5 s. No greeting, no channel intro. |
| Promise + roadmap | ~0:30 | One line saying what the viewer will get ("इस वीडियो के आख़िर तक आप जानेंगे कि असल में क्या हुआ था… और कौन-सी थ्योरी सबूतों पर खरी उतरती है"). A roadmap ("तीन सबूत, चार थ्योरी") gives a sense of progress. |
| Chapters | every 60–90 s | Each chapter answers one question and raises the next (the LEMMiNO "mystery novel" structure). Chapter title on screen. |
| Teasers | every 60–90 s | A forward-looking line: "पर असली झटका अभी बाक़ी है…", "ये बात अगले हिस्से में सब बदल देगी". Guides claim this lifts the 50%/75% marks noticeably. Use each phrase once. |
| Midpoint reset | ~50% | The biggest twist or a counter-intuitive fact, plus a change of format (new voice, graphic, music shift). The middle is where retention sags. |
| Theories / verdict | last third | Lay out theories against the evidence (✔/✘ scoreboard). Viewers compare and form an opinion: this is what makes a mystery "interactive". |
| Payoff | end | Answer the promise from the open (or the best answer), echo the first line. Short ending; no long outro. |

## Pace and picture (faceless)

- **The picture changes every 3–5 s** (cut, zoom, graphic, text). Ours: ~2.7 s per image with depth
  motion — fine. The problem is *sameness*, not speed: 7 min of one visual type.
- **Visual variety by type**, rotating: moving image → map/route animation → timeline → evidence
  board → document/photo close-up → date/temperature/name card → AI video clip. Change type when the
  idea changes and before a likely dip.
- **On-screen text for every key claim, number, date and name** (synced with the narration).
  Serves sound-off viewers; guides report large retention gains. Bigger text on mobile.
- **Sound:** shift the music at chapter changes and at the midpoint (not one track for 7 minutes);
  short silence before a reveal; ambience matching the scene. Never a sound effect on a voice
  onset (CLAUDE.md rule).
- **Pauses:** a deliberate pause after a surprising claim and before a reveal; cut accidental pauses
  over ~0.5 s.
- **Sentences under ~15 words**, active voice (ours averages ~13 per scene: OK).
- **A second voice:** short dramatized lines (hiker, investigator, villager) break the single-narrator
  monotony (dialogue-writer skill).

## Packaging and promotion

- Chapters in the description (we do this), with curiosity-driven titles.
- Cut a 30–60 s vertical teaser of the best moment as a Short / Reel that points to the full video.
- Engagement: a question to viewers ("आपके हिसाब से क्या हुआ था?") drives comments; only with the
  owner's OK (standing rule: no end-screen CTA).

## Dyatlov Pass audit (what to fix next time)

Has: cold open on the mystery, 8 chapters in the description, a theories section, short sentences.
Missing: promise/roadmap line, on-screen text/graphics (map, timeline, names, temperature), chapter
title cards in the video, teasers between chapters, a midpoint twist + format change, music changes
(one track, "Dark Fog", for the whole video), a second voice, any real motion. Also unknown: whether
the problem is click-through (77 views) or retention. Needs YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly)
to see the retention curve.

## Self-check for a long script

- [ ] First 5 s match the title/thumbnail; strangest fact in the first 30 s
- [ ] Promise + roadmap line by ~0:30
- [ ] Chapters every 60–90 s, each ending on a question
- [ ] A teaser line every 60–90 s, no phrase repeated
- [ ] A midpoint twist with a format change
- [ ] On-screen text planned for every number, date, name
- [ ] At least 4 visual types besides moving images
- [ ] Theories/verdict section; ending echoes the open
