### 1. Dyatlov Pass incident: `researched`

**Hook:** 9 experienced hikers, a tent cut open **from the inside**, and bodies found in the
snow, some barefoot, in −30 °C.

Fact sheet:
- ✅ Group of 9 ski-hikers led by Igor Dyatlov, northern Ural Mountains (Kholat Syakhl
  slope), night of 1–2 Feb 1959. All 9 died.
- ✅ Tent found cut open from the inside; footprints of people walking downhill in socks or
  barefoot; first bodies near a cedar tree at the forest edge in underwear.
- ✅ Later bodies (found in May in a ravine) had severe injuries: skull and chest fractures; one
  missing tongue and eyes (likely decomposition/scavengers in a stream, which is how to frame it).
- ⚠️ Traces of radioactivity on some clothing (reported; one victim worked with radioactive
  materials; this is disputed in meaning).
- ✅ Original Soviet verdict: "a compelling natural force". Case closed and files sealed, which
  fed conspiracy theories.
- ✅ 2019: Russian prosecutors reopened the case and concluded avalanche, without publishing
  full data.
- ✅ Jan 2021: Gaume & Puzrin (EPFL/ETH Zurich), *Communications Earth & Environment*. A small
  delayed **slab avalanche**, possibly triggered by the cut the group made into the slope for
  the tent, katabatic wind loading. Release estimated 9.5–13.5 h after camp was set. Injuries
  fit a heavy slab on people lying on skis. The authors themselves say they have **not**
  "solved" it; they also say the blunt injuries are not typical avalanche injuries.
- Theories to walk through (wildest → likeliest): Yeti/"menk", UFO/lights in the sky, secret
  weapons test, infrasound panic, local Mansi people (cleared at the time), avalanche.

Sources: [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/science/dyatlov-pass-incident-mystery-new-theory) ·
[Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/dyatlov-pass-incident-slab-avalanche-hypothesis.html) ·
[Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2021/01/28/study-sheds-new-light-on-russias-dyatlov-pass-mountain-mystery-a72771) ·
[CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/sci-tech/study-sheds-new-light-on-russia-s-dyatlov-pass-mystery-1.5286228)
Still to verify before scripting: exact temperatures, names/ages of the 9, the date bodies
were found (Feb vs May). Use Wikipedia's citations to reach primary sources.

Chapters (draft): Cold open: the cut tent → Who were the 9 → The last photos & diary →
Search party finds the tent → The bodies under the cedar → The ravine in May → "Compelling
natural force" & the sealed files → Theories → The avalanche study → What it still can't explain.

### 2. Mary Celeste: `researched`

**Hook:** a ship found sailing in the middle of the Atlantic: food, cargo and belongings on
board, **not a single person**.

- ✅ Found 4 Dec 1872 by the British brig *Dei Gratia* (Capt. David Morehouse), between the
  Azores and Portugal. Seaworthy; one lifeboat missing; last log entry unremarkable.
- ✅ Aboard when she left New York: Capt. Benjamin Briggs, his wife Sarah, their 2-year-old
  daughter Sophia, 7 crew. Never found.
- ✅ Cargo: ~1,700 barrels of alcohol; **9 found empty**. ~6 months of food untouched.
- ⚠️ Type of alcohol (denatured ethanol vs methanol): sources disagree; say "शराब बनाने वाला
  अल्कोहल नहीं, उद्योग वाला अल्कोहल" without naming the chemical.
- ✅ 2006, Dr Andrea Sella (UCL): a model hold with butane showed a vapour flash can leave **no
  burn marks**, supporting the "fumes scared them into the lifeboat, then the ship sailed
  away" theory. ⚠️ Critics: butane and paper, not alcohol and wood.
- Theories: sea monster/giant octopus → pirates → mutiny → insurance fraud by the *Dei Gratia*
  crew (salvage hearing in Gibraltar was suspicious) → alcohol vapour scare (likeliest).
- ❌ Myth: "warm tea still on the table, half-eaten breakfast". It came from Arthur Conan
  Doyle's 1884 fiction story ("Marie Celeste"). Good twist scene. (Verify the Conan Doyle
  detail before scripting.)

Sources: [UCL 2006](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2006/may/solved-mystery-mary-celeste) ·
[Explorersweb](https://explorersweb.com/exploration-mysteries-the-mary-celeste/) ·
[IFLScience](https://www.iflscience.com/in-1872-the-mary-celeste-was-found-completely-abandoned-what-happened-to-its-crew-69086) ·
[maryceleste.net on the cargo](https://maryceleste.net/denature.htm)

### 3. Flannan Isles lighthouse (Eilean Mòr): `researched`

**Hook:** 3 lighthouse keepers on a tiny island in the Atlantic, and one December day the
light simply went out.

- ✅ Keepers: James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, Donald MacArthur. Light reported dark by the
  steamer *Archtor* on 15 Dec 1900.
- ✅ Relief vessel *Hesperus* arrived only 26 Dec (weather). Relief keeper Joseph Moore found
  the gate and door closed, lamps cleaned and ready, kitchen tidy. Two sets of outdoor
  clothing gone, one left behind (so one man went out without his coat).
- ⚠️ "Overturned chair" (RD): appears in later retellings; not clearly in the official report.
- ✅ Superintendent Robert Muirhead's report (8 Jan 1901): storm damage at the west landing
  (a crane box torn away well above sea level, railings bent). Conclusion: the men were swept
  away by an extraordinarily large wave while securing equipment.
- ❌ Myth: dramatic log entries ("storm raging… Ducat irritable… MacArthur crying… God is over
  all") are later inventions. Verify against NRS before using; it is a good twist scene.
- Theories: sea serpent / ghost ship / "Phantom of the seven hunters" → spies kidnap →
  murder-suicide → freak wave (official).

Sources: [National Records of Scotland](https://nrscotland.gov.uk/learning-and-events/for-the-safety-of-all-scotlands-lighthouses/the-flannan-islands-lighthouse-a-disappearance/) ·
[History Hit](https://www.historyhit.com/the-flannan-isle-mystery-when-three-lighthouse-keepers-vanished-overnight/) ·
[Historic UK](https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofScotland/The-Eilean-Mor-Lighthouse-Mystery/) ·
[Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/mystery/115-year-old-mystery-flannan-lighthouses-missing-keepers)

### 4. Sodder children: `researched`

**Hook:** a house burns down on Christmas night; 5 children are "dead", yet no bones are
ever found.

- ✅ Fayetteville, West Virginia. Fire in the early hours of **Christmas Day** 1945 (RD says
  Christmas Eve: use "क्रिसमस की रात"). George & Jennie Sodder and 4 children escaped; 5
  children (Maurice, Martha, Louis, Jennie, Betty) were never seen again.
- ✅ Ladder missing, phone line cut, the truck wouldn't start. Fire ruled accidental
  (electrical); no remains found. A crematorium noted that bones normally survive such a fire.
- ✅ 1952: the family put up a **billboard** with the children's photos and a reward on Route
  16. It stood until after Jennie's death in 1989.
- ⚠️ Sightings, the 1967 photo of "Louis" mailed to the family, the Mussolini angle: all
  unproven. Present them as what the family believed.
- Tone: real victims, siblings' descendants are alive. No named culprit.

Sources: [Wikipedia (follow citations)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodder_children_disappearance) ·
[NPR 2005](https://www.npr.org/2005/12/23/5067563/mystery-of-missing-children-haunts-w-va-town) ·
[The Collector](https://www.thecollector.com/mystery-sodder-children/)

### 6. Tunguska event: `idea` (partly researched)

- ✅ 30 June 1908, near the Podkamennaya Tunguska River, Siberia. ~2,150 km², ~80 million trees
  flattened. No crater.
- ✅ Eyewitness at the Vanavara trading post (~65 km away) felt heat "as if my shirt was on
  fire" and was thrown from his chair (testimony recorded by Kulik's team).
- ✅ Leonid Kulik reached the site in 1927 with Evenki guides: trees flattened outward in a
  radial pattern; trees at the centre standing but stripped. ⚠️ The "butterfly" shape of the
  fall was mapped from later aerial surveys: verify the date.
- Explanation: airburst of a stony asteroid or comet fragment a few km up. Alternatives:
  comet, black hole, antimatter, Tesla (fun "wild theories" block).

Sources: [APS News](https://aps.org/apsnews/2018/06/tunguska-event) ·
[Slate: eyewitness accounts](https://www.slate.com/blogs/the_vault/2013/02/15/tunguska_event_eyewitness_accounts_from_the_last_time_a_heavenly_body_burned.html)
